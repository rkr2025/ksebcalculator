🧭 Eisenhower Matrix

Sort tasks by urgency and importance to decide what to do now, schedule, delegate, or delete.

❓ How This Works

Urgent means a task demands your immediate attention -- there are clear consequences for delaying it. Important means it moves you toward your long-term goals, even if it doesn't feel pressing today.

  • 🔥 Do First (urgent & important): handle these right away.
  • 📅 Schedule (important, not urgent): give these a future date and keep them on track.
  • 🤝 Delegate (urgent, not important): hand these off if you can.
  • 🗑️ Delete (neither): drop these, or write down why you won't do them.

Try to keep each quadrant to around 10 tasks so it stays useful at a glance, and review it regularly -- tasks drift between quadrants as deadlines get closer. Watch out for the "mere-urgency effect": it's easy to let loud, urgent-but-unimportant tasks crowd out the quiet, important work.
